BAS Pipeline: Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026

BAS Pipeline is a private tool operated by ReGen Labs ("we"). It is used only by the staff and advisers of the client business it serves, to read that business's own supplier invoices and prepare the figures for its Business Activity Statement (BAS). This page explains what the tool accesses and what it does with that information.

What the tool accesses

  • Gmail, read-only: one mailbox that receives the client's supplier invoices. The tool reads email and attachments in that mailbox. It cannot send, change or delete anything in it.
  • Google Drive, only files the tool creates: a Google Sheet that shows the results. The tool cannot see any other file in Drive.
  • Sending email, from one separate account: short notices to the people who look after the tool, for example "credit is running low" or "sign-in needs renewing", and a weekly summary. It sends nothing to anyone else.

What we do with the information

  • We pick out supplier invoices and record the invoice date, supplier, invoice number, GST and total.
  • Invoice documents that need reading are sent to Anthropic's API, which returns those figures. Documents from senders marked as not to be processed (such as payroll) are never sent.
  • Results are kept in a database on a server we control and shown in the Google Sheet, which is shared only with the client's staff and accountant.
  • We use Google user data only to provide this tool. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, use it for advertising, or let people read it except as described here. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

We keep invoices and results for as long as the client needs them for its tax and business records, and delete them when the client asks.

Your choices

You can stop the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask us to delete data, email us at the address below.
