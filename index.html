BAS Pipeline

BAS Pipeline is a private, internal tool used by Cardwell Grocers Pty Ltd to read the business's own supplier invoices from its own mailbox and prepare the figures needed for its Business Activity Statement (BAS): invoice date, supplier, invoice number, GST and total.

It is not a public service and is not offered to other businesses or the public. The only people who sign in to it are the staff and advisers of Cardwell Grocers Pty Ltd.

Read how the tool handles data in the privacy policy.
