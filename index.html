BAS Pipeline

BAS Pipeline is a private tool operated by ReGen Labs. It reads a client business's own supplier invoices from that business's own mailbox and prepares the figures needed for its Business Activity Statement (BAS): invoice date, supplier, invoice number, GST and total.

It is not a public service and is not offered to the public. The only people who sign in to it are the staff and advisers of the business it serves.

Read how the tool handles data in the privacy policy.
